Welcome

Edit the website yourself.

Everything on the Mjazo website can be changed at mjazo.vercel.app/admin, no code needed: services and prices, every page, menus, the blog, help articles, legal text, pictures, SEO and the Urdu site. Customers see nothing until someone publishes.

Your first edit in five steps

  1. Sign in at /admin with your email. Owners and Admins also type the code from their authenticator app.
  2. Find the thing to change in the menu on the left. Or open Live preview and click the text you want to edit.
  3. Make your change. It saves by itself as a draft a moment after you stop typing.
  4. Check it with Preview, which shows the real page with your draft, only to you.
  5. Publish. The website updates straight away. Changed your mind? History brings back any earlier version.

  • Use your own account. The activity log records who changed what.

Your account

Signing in, and who can do what

Signing in

Go to mjazo.vercel.app/admin. Sign in with your email and password, or click Email me a sign-in link and open the link from your inbox. Forgot password? sends a link to set a new one.

New people are invited by an Owner or Admin from Team → People & roles. Open the invitation email, then set a password under Account (top of the menu).

Links can take a few minutes to arrive. Ask for one, then wait; asking again and again slows them down.

Two-step sign-in

Owners and Admins must use it. Everyone else can turn it on under Account. You need an authenticator app on your phone: Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or 1Password.

  1. Scan the QR code the admin shows you.
  2. Type the 6-digit code from the app.
  3. Each time you sign in, type the new code.

Lost your phone? Ask an Owner to reset your two-step sign-in from People & roles, then set it up again.

Editing

Edit, check, publish

Drafts save themselves

A moment after you stop typing, your changes are saved as a draft and the top of the page says Draft saved. Close the tab and come back later; the draft waits for you.

Save a version: Ctrl S

On a Mac, ⌘ S. This saves now and keeps this version in History, so you can come back to it. Do it before a big rewrite.

Preview

Opens the real page with your draft in a new tab. Only you see it. Check it on your phone as well as a computer.

Live preview and click-to-edit

Live preview shows the page beside the form and updates as you type. Click any text in it to jump straight to its field.

Publish

Puts your draft on the website straight away. Every publish is kept in History.

Schedule publish

Pick a date and time (Karachi time). The draft publishes by itself, once an Owner has finished setting this up (the Dashboard says if not). If someone who can't publish edits it in the meantime, the schedule is cancelled so nothing unchecked goes live.

Submit for review (Authors)

Authors can't publish. Submit for review puts the draft on the Dashboard under Waiting for you for an Editor to check and publish.

Discard changes

Throws the draft away. The live version stays as it is.

Hide from site

Takes an item off the website, for example a service you've stopped offering. Restore to site brings it back.

Two people, one item

If someone else saved the same item while you were editing, you get a warning instead of overwriting their work. Reload to see their changes, then make yours again.

Before you publish

  • Preview on a phone: most customers book from one.
  • Prices: check the amount and the "from" wording. Only Owners and Admins can change them.
  • If the Urdu site is switched on, check the Urdu too (page 8).

Nothing is lost

History, activity and backups

History

Every item has a History button. It lists every publish, every Ctrl S save, every restore and every import, with who did it and when.

  1. Click Compare on a version.
  2. Choose what to compare it with: what's in the form now, or the live version. Only the differences show, word by word: struck-out words are in the old version, highlighted words are newer.
  3. To go back, click Restore. The old version becomes your draft. Publish it to make it live.

Activity log

Team → Activity log shows who published, scheduled, hid, restored, invited or downloaded what, and when. Use it to answer "who changed this price?".

Backups

Download (Owners and Admins)

Team → Backup → Download backup gives you one file with everything people have saved here: pages, catalogue, posts, settings (live, drafts, scheduled and hidden), the media library's records and the redirects.

Download one before big changes and keep it somewhere private: it holds all the site's content.

Not in a backup: the built-in content (it ships with the site) and the photo files themselves (they stay in storage).

Restore (Owners only)

Choose the file, check the summary, then pick:

  • As drafts (recommended): every entry comes back as a draft. The website doesn't change until someone reviews and publishes.
  • Exactly as in the backup: live content, drafts, schedules and hidden items become what they were. The website changes straight away.

A restore never deletes anything. Entries that already match are left alone, and every restored entry gets an "Imported" version in its History.

Page builder

New pages from blocks

For a campaign, an event or a landing page, build a page from ready-made blocks in Mjazo's style. No designer needed.

  1. Pages → Block pages → New. Give it a title and an address, for example /campaigns/eid-sale: lowercase, words joined with hyphens.
  2. Add blocks with the + between them. Drag the handle to reorder, or use Duplicate and Remove.
  3. Turn on Live preview to see the page as you build it, then Publish.

Tips for a good-looking page

  • Each block has a background: Paper, Ink, Saffron or Blush. Alternate them, and use Saffron at most once.
  • Put *one word* of a headline in stars to make it the italic accent: A headline for this page.
  • Change a published page's address and the old one redirects to it by itself.

Pictures and Urdu

Media, and the Urdu site

Pictures and media

Upload in Media, or straight from any image field. Accepted: JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF and GIF up to 10 MB; MP4 and WebM up to 50 MB; 3D models (GLB) up to 25 MB; PDF up to 10 MB.

Alt text

Describe what the picture shows, for blind visitors and for Google: "A Mjazo pro threading a customer's brows at home". The AI can suggest one; it's marked Written by AI. Check it before saving.

Focal point

Click the important part of the picture, such as a face. When the site crops the picture for phones, that part stays in view.

Replace file

Swaps the file but keeps it in place. Every page using it shows the new one, so there's no need to edit each page.

Before deleting

Each item shows where it's used. Delete only what says Not used anywhere yet.

Our pictures: real Karachi homes and real pros. Never retouch or lighten skin.

Urdu

Every text field has an Urdu side. Click اردو at the top of an editor to show the Urdu fields next to the English ones.

AI translation

Translate the rest with AI in an editor fills the empty Urdu fields. Each one carries a purple AI translation, needs review label. Read them, correct what's wrong, then click Mark AI Urdu as reviewed.

Tools → Urdu translation

Shows how much of the site has Urdu, and can translate everything still missing in one go.

Going public

The Urdu site at /ur goes public when an Owner or Admin switches it on in Settings → Feature switches. Before that, a native speaker reads the Urdu, especially prices, policies and anything legal.

Changing the English doesn't change the Urdu. When you change what an English sentence says, update its Urdu too.

Being found

SEO, addresses and special text

SEO

Most items have an SEO section: the title and description Google shows, and the picture shown when someone shares the link on WhatsApp. It previews the Google result and gives a score with tips.

Tools → SEO lists every page with its score, the checks that failed and its speed. Once an Owner has switched it on, the whole site is checked again by itself every Monday.

Addresses and redirects

When the address of a service, post or page changes, the old address redirects to the new one by itself, so old links and Google keep working.

Tools → Redirects is for anything else, such as a short address for a campaign (/eid → /campaigns/eid-sale). It warns about addresses that lead nowhere.

Special text

Placeholders: {{ … }}

Some text has placeholders, such as {{policy.freeChangeHours}}. The site fills them in from Settings, so a change there updates the text everywhere. Keep the double braces exactly as they are.

WriteShows
{{catalog.serviceCount}}How many services there are
{{catalog.areaCount}}How many Karachi areas are live
{{catalog.areaNames}}Their names
{{policy.freeChangeHours}}Hours for free changes
{{plus.priceText}}The Plus price

The italic accent: *word*

In headlines, one word in stars becomes the italic accent. Use it once per headline.

House style

Writing for Mjazo

  • Warm, confident, a little cheeky, never cheesy. Short sentences. Start with the verb: "Book a facial", not "Facials can be booked".
  • English with Roman Urdu touches; Urdu where it helps the customer.
  • Say clear prices, never "cheap".
  • No fake numbers, reviews or urgency. No "only 2 slots left" unless it's true.
  • No whitening or fairness words. Say "brightening facial".
  • When a customer is talking to an AI, say so.
