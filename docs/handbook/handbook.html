Welcome

Edit the website yourself.

Everything on the Mjazo website can be changed at mjazo.vercel.app/admin, no code needed: services and prices, every page, menus, the blog, help articles, legal text, pictures, SEO and the Urdu site. Customers see nothing until someone publishes.

The pictures in this handbook are real screens of the admin. The numbered circles on each one are explained in the list under it.

Your first edit in five steps

  1. Sign in at /admin with your email. Owners and Admins also type the code from their authenticator app (page 3).
  2. Find the thing to change in the menu on the left (page 5). Or open Live preview and click the text you want to edit (page 8).
  3. Make your change. It saves by itself as a draft a moment after you stop typing.
  4. Check it with Preview, which shows the real page with your draft, only to you.
  5. Publish. The website updates straight away. Changed your mind? History brings back any earlier version (page 10).

Three things to remember

  • Drafts are private. Only Publish changes the website.
  • Nothing is lost: every publish is kept in History, and every save you make with Ctrl S.
  • Use your own account. The activity log records who changed what.

Your account

Signing in

Go to mjazo.vercel.app/admin. New people get an invitation email from an Owner or Admin; open it, and you're in.

  1. Your email address.
  2. Your password, if you've set one (under Account, at the bottom of the menu).
  3. Sign in.
  4. Email me a sign-in link: no password needed. Open the link from your inbox on the same device. An Owner's very first sign-in works this way.
  5. Forgot password? emails a link to set a new one.

Links can take a few minutes to arrive. Ask for one, then wait: asking again and again slows them down.

Two-step sign-in

Owners and Admins must use it; everyone else can turn it on under Account. You need an authenticator app on your phone: Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or 1Password.

Setting it up (once)

  1. After signing in, the admin shows a QR code.
  2. In the app, add an account and scan it. "Mjazo CMS" appears with a 6-digit code that changes every 30 seconds.
  3. Type the code into the admin. Done.

Every sign-in after that

  1. Sign in with your email as usual.
  2. Open the app and type the code it shows for Mjazo CMS.

Lost your phone? Ask an Owner to reset your two-step sign-in from People & roles, then set it up again on your new phone.

Never share your account or your codes. Everyone gets their own sign-in, so the activity log can show who changed what.

Editing

Saving and live preview

Drafts save themselves

A moment after you stop typing, your changes are saved as a draft. Close the tab and come back later; the draft waits for you. If you change something back to exactly what's live, the draft disappears and the item shows as Live again.

Save a version: Ctrl S

On a Mac, ⌘ S. It saves now and keeps this version in History, to come back to. Do it before a big rewrite.

Preview

Opens the real page with your draft in a new tab. Only you see it.

Live preview and click-to-edit

  1. Live preview on or off. The admin remembers your choice.
  2. See the page at Desktop or Phone width.
  3. The English or اردو page.
  4. Click to edit: click any text in the preview to jump to its field.
  5. The real page with your draft. It updates a moment after each change. The icons above it reload it, open it in a new tab, or close it.

Editing

Publishing and scheduling

Publish

Puts your draft on the website straight away. Every publish is kept in History.

Schedule publish

  1. Pick the date and time, in Karachi time.
  2. Schedule. The item shows as Scheduled and publishes by itself.
  3. Cancel closes the form without scheduling.

A scheduled item shows Goes live with the time, and a Cancel schedule button. If someone who can't publish edits it in the meantime, the schedule is cancelled, so nothing unchecked goes live.

Scheduling works once an Owner has finished setting it up; the Dashboard says if not.

Submit for review (Authors)

Authors can't publish. Submit for review puts the draft on the Dashboard under Waiting for you for an Editor to check and publish.

Discard changes

Throws the draft away. The live version stays as it is.

Hide from site

Takes an item off the website, for example a service you've stopped offering. Restore to site brings it back.

Two people, one item

If someone else saved the same item while you were editing, you get a warning instead of overwriting their work. Reload, then make your change again.

Before you publish

  • Preview on a phone: most customers book from one.
  • Prices: check the amount and the "from" wording. Only Owners and Admins can change them.
  • If the Urdu site is switched on, check the Urdu too (page 15).

Nothing is lost

History and the activity log

Click History in any editor. It lists every publish, every Ctrl S save, every restore and every import, with who did it and when.

  1. What History keeps.
  2. Compare a version with now.
  3. Restore it as your draft.
  1. Compare with what's in the form now…
  2. …or with the live version.
  3. Red, struck out: in the old version. Green: newer.
  4. Restore that version as your draft, then publish it.

Activity log (Team → Activity log)

  1. Who did it.
  2. What they did: published, scheduled, hid, uploaded, invited, downloaded a backup…
  3. Details: which fields changed. Use it to answer "who changed this price?".

Nothing is lost

Backups

Team → Backup. Download one before big changes, and keep it somewhere private: it holds all the site's content.

  1. Download backup (Owners and Admins): one file with everything people have saved here.
  2. Choose a backup file to restore (Owners only).

What's in a backup

Every page, catalogue item, post and setting people have saved (live, drafts, scheduled and hidden), the media library's records and the redirects.

Not in it: the built-in content (it ships with the site) and the photo files themselves (they stay in storage).

Restoring

Choose the file and check the summary it shows, then pick:

  • As drafts (recommended): every entry comes back as a draft. The website doesn't change until someone reviews and publishes.
  • Exactly as in the backup: live content, drafts, schedules and hidden items become what they were. The website changes straight away; you tick a box to confirm.

A restore never deletes anything. Entries that already match are left alone, and every restored entry gets an "Imported" version in its History.

Page builder

Block pages

For a campaign, an event or a landing page: build a new page from ready-made blocks in Mjazo's style. Start with Pages → Block pages → New, give it a name and an address like /campaigns/eid-sale (lowercase, words joined with hyphens).

  1. The page's blocks, top to bottom.
  2. Drag a block by its handle to move it.
  3. Click a block to open it and edit its text, pictures, buttons and background.
  4. Duplicate a block.
  5. Remove a block.
  6. Open all blocks at once.
  7. This page is scheduled: it goes live by itself at that time.

Change a published page's address and the old address redirects to the new one by itself.

Pictures

Pictures and media

  1. Search by name, alt text or tag.
  2. Show only images, videos, 3D models or PDFs.
  3. Upload, or drop files anywhere on the page. JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, GIF and PDF up to 10 MB; MP4 and WebM video up to 50 MB; 3D models up to 25 MB.
  4. No alt text: add a description before using it.

Click a picture to open its details:

  1. Focal point: click the important part, such as a face. When the site crops the picture for phones, that part stays in view.
  2. Copy link to the file.
  3. Suggest with AI writes alt text from the picture. Check it before saving.
  4. Alt text: what the picture shows, for blind visitors and Google. "A Mjazo pro threading a customer's brows at home", in English and Urdu.
  5. Tags, to find it again.
  6. Where it's used: every page that shows it.
  7. Replace file: swaps the picture everywhere it's used at once.
  8. Delete: only possible when it isn't used anywhere.

Our pictures: real Karachi homes and real pros. Never retouch or lighten skin.

Being found

SEO in the editor

Most items have an SEO section near the bottom of the form: how the page appears in Google and when its link is shared on WhatsApp. Empty fields use the page's own text.

  1. Google: the result as Google shows it.
  2. WhatsApp & social: the card a shared link shows.
  3. The search title and description.
  4. Checks: green is fine, amber says what to fix.
  5. Suggest with AI: a title, description and keyword to start from.
  6. The page's latest SEO score.

The fields below the checks

Search titleShown in Google and the browser tab, 30 to 60 characters. "· Mjazo" is added for you.
Search descriptionThe grey text under the title in Google, 70 to 160 characters.
Focus keywordThe phrase the page should rank for, such as "waxing at home Karachi". The score checks it.
Share imageShown when the link is shared. A JPG or PNG of 1200 × 630 works best.
Hide from search enginesFor pages that shouldn't appear in Google.

Being found

The SEO dashboard

Tools → SEO. How every page scores in search, and what to fix first.

  1. The average SEO score, and how many pages are good, need work or are poor.
  2. Audit all pages: checks every page again (a few minutes).
  3. Search pages, addresses or keywords.
  4. Filter by section or problem, and sort.
  5. Each page's score: green 80+, amber 50 to 79, red under 50.
  6. Top problems to fix on that page.
  7. Audit one page again after you fix it. The pencil opens its editor.

An audit reads the published page the way Google does and runs 16 checks: title and description, focus keyword, headings, links, image alt text, amount of text and readability. Drafts count once they're published. Once an Owner has switched it on, the whole site is checked again by itself every Monday.

Redirects and special text

  1. Old address, for example /eid.
  2. New address: a page on the site or a full web address.
  3. Permanent for pages that moved for good.
  4. Add redirect. It warns about loops and addresses that lead nowhere.
  5. Test where any address ends up.
  6. Redirects added by themselves when an address changed.
  7. Redirects built into the site, set by a developer.

Placeholders: {{ … }}

Some text holds placeholders the site fills in from Settings, so one change there updates the text everywhere. Keep the double braces exactly as they are.

{{catalog.serviceCount}} services · {{catalog.areaCount}} live areas · {{policy.freeChangeHours}} free-change hours · {{plus.priceText}} the Plus price.

The italic accent: *word*

In headlines, one word in stars becomes the italic accent. Use it once per headline.

House style

Writing for Mjazo

  • Warm, confident, a little cheeky, never cheesy. Short sentences. Start with the verb: "Book a facial", not "Facials can be booked".
  • English with Roman Urdu touches; Urdu where it helps the customer.
  • Say clear prices, never "cheap".
  • No fake numbers, reviews or urgency. No "only 2 slots left" unless it's true.
  • No whitening or fairness words. Say "brightening facial".
  • When a customer is talking to an AI, say so.
